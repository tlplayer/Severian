# Contributing to Severian

By contributing to Severian, you agree that your contribution is made to the Severian project and is not retained as individually controlled project intellectual property.

This policy applies to all contributions, including code, commits, pull requests, patches, documentation, tests, benchmarks, examples, compiler changes, language design, specifications, proposals, issues, discussions, reviews, comments, designs, and other materials submitted for incorporation into or use by Severian.

## Ownership of Contributions

By submitting a contribution, you represent that you have the legal right to submit it and agree to assign to the owner of the Severian project all right, title, and interest you hold in the contribution, including all transferable copyright and other intellectual-property rights associated with it.

To the extent that any applicable right cannot legally be assigned, you grant the Severian project owner an exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, transferable, sublicensable, royalty-free license to use, reproduce, modify, distribute, publish, relicense, commercialize, enforce, and otherwise exploit that contribution for any purpose.

Where legally permitted, you waive and agree not to assert moral rights or similar rights that would restrict the Severian project's use or modification of the contribution.

Contributions become part of Severian. Contributors do not acquire ownership, governance rights, licensing authority, veto rights, commercialization rights, or other rights over Severian by contributing.

## Project Licensing

The Severian project owner retains sole authority to determine how Severian and its components are licensed, distributed, relicensed, commercialized, or otherwise made available.

A contribution does not restrict the project owner from:

* changing Severian's license;
* offering different licenses to different users;
* offering commercial licenses;
* making portions of Severian proprietary;
* incorporating contributions into other Severian products or services;
* transferring or assigning ownership of Severian;
* modifying, replacing, or removing contributed work.

No contributor approval is required for these actions.

## No Continuing Control

After submission and acceptance, a contributor cannot require that their contribution:

* remain in Severian;
* retain its original implementation or design;
* remain under a particular license;
* remain publicly available;
* be attributed beyond requirements imposed by applicable law;
* not be commercially exploited;
* not be relicensed;
* not be modified or replaced.

The Severian project may modify, combine, refactor, translate, compile, distribute, commercialize, relicense, remove, or otherwise use accepted contributions without further permission from the contributor.

## Discussions and Language Design

Ideas, proposals, specifications, syntax designs, APIs, algorithms, architectural proposals, documentation, and other materials intentionally submitted through official Severian contribution channels may be used by the Severian project without restriction to the maximum extent permitted by law.

Participation in a discussion does not grant a participant ownership or control over a Severian language feature, specification, API, implementation, or design derived from that discussion.

Nothing in this section claims ownership over a contributor's pre-existing intellectual property merely because it was mentioned during a discussion.

## Contributor Representations

By contributing, you represent that:

1. you created the contribution or otherwise have sufficient authority to contribute it under these terms;
2. the contribution does not knowingly violate another party's intellectual-property rights;
3. your employer or another party does not own rights that prevent you from making the contribution;
4. you have disclosed any known third-party licensing requirements applicable to the contribution; and
5. you understand that an accepted contribution may be used commercially.

Do not submit material that you do not have authority to contribute.

## Third-Party Code

Third-party code remains governed by its applicable license and is not converted into Severian-owned intellectual property merely by being included in a contribution.

Contributors must clearly identify third-party code, dependencies, generated material, or other content that they did not create otherwise it is considered a contribution and the contributor bares the responsibility.

## Acceptance of These Terms

Submitting a pull request, commit, patch, or other contribution to Severian constitutes agreement to these contribution terms.

Where the project requires a Contributor License Agreement, copyright assignment, Developer Certificate of Origin, or equivalent confirmation, the contributor must also complete that process before the contribution can be accepted.

Contributions that cannot be accepted under these terms may be rejected.

## No Obligation to Accept Contributions

Submission does not require the Severian project to review, merge, maintain, distribute, or otherwise use a contribution.

The Severian project owner retains final authority over acceptance and use of contributions.
